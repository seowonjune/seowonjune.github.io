I am an undergraduate student in Computer Science and Engineering at **UNIST** (Ulsan National Institute of Science and Technology), where I work with Prof. Saerom Park in the **Safe AI Lab**.

My research looks at how deployed AI systems actually fail, and how to make them systems that people can trust and keep under control. I have worked on this from both the attack and defense sides, across generative models and large language models (LLMs). Going forward, I am especially interested in:

- **Rethinking safety evaluation for deployment.** What assumptions do current safety evaluations rely on, and when do they break once a model is quantized, fine-tuned, or deployed as an agent with tools and permissions?
- **Safety by design.** Instead of patching failures after the fact, can we build safety into models during training so that it holds up under deployment-time changes and adversarial pressure?

Previously, I co-founded a deepfake-prevention startup, where I led the development of protective technology against deepfake generation.

**I am applying to PhD programs for Fall 2027.**
