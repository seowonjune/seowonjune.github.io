MIG-COW combines momentum-integrated gradients with a consensus-orthogonal decomposition to capture both shared and model-specific vulnerabilities across heterogeneous CNN and ViT deepfake detectors.

It achieves a 99.96% white-box attack success rate with high perceptual similarity on the AADD-2025 Challenge benchmarks and placed second in the challenge.
